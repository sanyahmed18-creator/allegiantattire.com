Custom Hoodies Dubai: 2026 Price Guide, GSM Comparison & Minimum Order

BUYING GUIDE · OCT 6, 2026 · 8 MIN READ

Hoodies are the single most popular custom garment in the UAE — not just for winter, but year-round for air-conditioned offices, gym sessions, event merch and brand drops. If you are pricing custom hoodies in Dubai for the first time (or re-ordering after a bad experience), this guide covers everything: GSM weights, fleece types, print methods, realistic pricing and the questions that separate good factories from bad ones.

Understanding GSM: Why Weight Matters More Than You Think

GSM (grams per square meter) measures fabric weight. For hoodies, this directly affects warmth, drape and perceived quality. Here is how the numbers translate to real-world use in the UAE:

GSM Range Feel Best For Price Range (AED/pc)
250–280 GSM Light, thin Budget events, giveaways, gym layering 75–95
300–320 GSM Standard retail weight Corporate merch, brand drops, school 85–110
350–380 GSM Heavy, structured Premium merch, streetwear, retail resale 95–125
400+ GSM Extra heavy, boxy Luxury drops, oversized streetwear 105–145
💡 Pro tip: Always weigh the sample yourself. A quoted 350 GSM hoodie that arrives at 310 GSM is a different product entirely. Ask for a fabric certificate before approving the bulk run.

Fleece Types: Brushed vs French Terry vs Garment-Washed

The inside of the hoodie matters as much as the outside. Three main fleece constructions dominate the UAE market:

  • Brushed fleece (terry loop brushed): Soft, fluffy interior. The most common choice for hoodies in Dubai. Feels warm without being heavy.
  • French terry: Smooth loop interior, no brushing. Better for activewear and year-round UAE use — breathes more than brushed fleece.
  • Garment-washed: Pre-washed for a vintage, broken-in feel. Adds AED 8–15 per piece but gives the hoodie a premium, lived-in texture from day one.

Print Methods for Hoodies: What Works on Thick Fabric

Hoodies present a printing challenge that t-shirts do not: the fabric is thick, textured and often has a brushed surface that resists adhesion. Here is what actually works:

DTF Printing

AED 14–22/pc
  • Full-colour, no minimum
  • Works on any colour hoodie
  • Same-day available
  • 30+ wash cycles
  • Best for: urgent, multi-colour, small runs

Screen Printing

AED 7–14/pc
  • Setup cost per colour
  • Best above 100 pieces
  • 50+ wash cycles
  • Pantone-matched inks
  • Best for: bulk, 1–3 colours

Embroidery

AED 11–18/pc
  • Premium textured look
  • 500+ wash durability
  • Best on chest, sleeve
  • Not for large all-over art
  • Best for: logos, corporate, caps

Vinyl / Heat Transfer

AED 8–12/pc
  • Names and numbers
  • Single colour marks
  • Fastest for team kits
  • Limited to simple designs
  • Best for: sports teams, numbering

How to Get the Best Price on Custom Hoodies

  1. Know your GSM. Do not let a supplier talk you into a lighter weight to "save money" — it will feel cheap and your team/audience will notice.
  2. Choose the right print method. DTF for small runs and full colour. Screen print for 100+ with 1–3 colours. Embroidery for logos that need to last years.
  3. Order in standard sizes. Custom size curves (e.g., all XS or all 3XL) add cost. A standard S-M-L-XL-XXL spread is cheapest.
  4. Combine colours carefully. Each additional colourway in a cut-and-sew run adds a fabric minimum. Stick to 1–2 colours unless you have volume.
  5. Reorder from the same factory. Your pattern, fabric and colour specs are already on file. Re-orders are faster and often 5–10% cheaper.

Turnaround Times for Custom Hoodies

  • Sample: 5–7 working days
  • Bulk production: 15–20 working days (2–3 weeks)
  • Re-order (same spec, same factory): 10–14 working days
  • Rush DTF on stock blanks: Same day for quantities under 50

Common Mistakes When Ordering Custom Hoodies

  • Not asking for a physical sample. A digital mockup tells you nothing about hand feel, print adhesion or true colour. Always get a sample first.
  • Ignoring the zip quality. Cheap zips fail in 3 months. Ask for YKK or equivalent metal zips on full-zip hoodies.
  • Ordering the lightest weight to save money. A 250 GSM "hoodie" is basically a long-sleeve t-shirt with a hood. It will not drape well or keep anyone warm.
  • Not specifying ribbed cuffs and hem. Some factories skip the ribbing to cut costs. The hoodie stretches out and loses shape after a few washes.
  • Forgetting about printing on the back. If you want art on both front and back, say so upfront — it changes the print method and price.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a custom hoodie cost in Dubai?
A custom-printed hoodie in Dubai costs AED 83–130 per piece depending on GSM weight, print method and order quantity. A 350 GSM hoodie with DTF printing typically costs AED 95–110 at 24+ pieces. Premium 400 GSM garment-washed hoodies run AED 100–130.
What is the minimum order for custom hoodies in Dubai?
Most UAE factories accept custom hoodie orders from 12 pieces per style. Cut-and-sew custom hoodies with your own fabric start at 100 pieces per colour.
What GSM should I choose for custom hoodies?
280–320 GSM is standard for retail-quality hoodies in the UAE climate. 350–400 GSM is premium heavyweight — ideal for winter drops, corporate merch and retail. Below 280 GSM feels thin and is best for budget event giveaways only.
Which print method is best for hoodies?
DTF printing is best for full-colour designs under 100 pieces. Screen printing wins on cost above 100 pieces with 1–3 colours. Embroidery is best for a premium look on the chest or sleeve. Vinyl heat transfer works for names and numbers on team hoodies.
