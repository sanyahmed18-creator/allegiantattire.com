        FAQ — STRAIGHT ANSWERS

        BEFORE YOU ORDER.

        What is your minimum order quantity?

        DTF & print services start at 1 pc. Custom blanks start at 10–12 pcs per style; cut-and-sew factory runs start at 100 pcs per colour.

        How fast can you deliver in Dubai?

        Same-day DTF pressing on ready blanks, 5–7 days for sampling, 2–3 weeks for bulk. Dubai courier is same-day, 1–2 days across the UAE.

        Do you provide samples before bulk?

        Yes — a pre-production sample with your artwork and labels is approved before the bulk run starts, usually within 5 days.

        Which print method should I choose?

        DTF for full-colour, urgent or low quantities; screen print for large single-colour runs; sublimation for all-over activewear; embroidery for polos, caps and uniforms.

        Can you handle labelling & packaging?

        Woven neck labels, size chips, hang tags, poly-bags and outer cartons — printed, sewn and packed in-house.
